# Privacy Policy for FuelWise Studio

**Effective Date:** November 4, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 11, 2026  

FuelWise Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application **FuelWise Studio** (`com.lopup9999.eurofuelapp`).

---

### 1. Information We Collect

* **Location Data:** We request access to your device’s precise location (GPS) solely to detect nearby fuel stations, calculate distance, and display optimized fuel prices in your immediate area. Location data is processed locally on your device and sent securely over encrypted connections to our backend API to fetch station results. **We do not store, track, or retain your location history or movement patterns.**
* **Technical Logs and Performance Data:** When accessing our backend service, standard non-identifying technical data (such as API request timestamps, anonymous device parameters, IP address, and app version) may be processed transiently by our servers to maintain service uptime, prevent abuse, and optimize spatial query performance.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services & Entitlements

We integrate trusted third-party service providers to power map displays, manage access entitlements, and process app distribution:

* **RevenueCat:** Manages in-app purchase entitlements and subscription states. RevenueCat processes anonymous app user identifiers to verify purchase status.
* **Apple App Store & Google Play Services:** Handle all financial transactions, payment processing, and application updates securely. We never collect, store, or have access to your credit card or payment billing information.
* **OpenStreetMap & Map Tile Providers:** Render vector/raster map tiles within the app interface.
* **Backend Infrastructure (Render & Supabase):** Hosts our spatial backend (FastAPI / PostGIS) to serve real-time fuel pricing data over HTTPS.

---

### 3. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

* **No Personal Accounts:** We do not require account registration, login credentials, user names, or email addresses to use the app.
* **Security:** All communications between the app and backend APIs are strictly encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS protocols.
* **Data Selling:** We **do not sell, rent, or trade** any user data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

---

### 4. User Rights and Data Control

* **Location Permissions:** You can grant or revoke location permissions at any time through your device system settings (`Settings > Privacy > Location Services`). Revoking access will prevent the automatic calculation of nearby fuel prices based on your live location.
* **Data Access & Deletion:** Because we do not collect user accounts or persistent identity records, we do not hold identifiable personal data. For any privacy-related inquiries, you can contact us directly at the email listed below.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy

FuelWise Studio is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

# Privacy Policy for FuelWise Studio

**Effective Date:** November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FuelWise Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information for our mobile application **FuelWise Studio** (`com.lopup9999.eurofuelapp`).

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Location Data:** We request access to your device's precise location (GPS) solely to detect nearby fuel stations, calculate distances, and show fuel prices in your immediate area. Location data is processed locally on your device and sent securely to our API to fetch nearby stations. We do not store or track your historical location movements.
- **Diagnostics and App Performance:** We may process technical log data and device parameters to ensure service stability, API uptime, and performance optimization.

## 2. Third-Party Services
Our app integrates third-party tools to deliver core functionality:
- **RevenueCat:** Used to manage in-app entitlements and process non-renewable prepaid access.
- **OpenStreetMap & Map Tiles:** Used to render map displays.
- **Google Play Services:** Used to facilitate secure app distribution and in-app transactions.

## 3. Data Retention and Security
All API communications are encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. We do not collect, retain, or sell personal user accounts, names, or addresses.

## 4. Children's Privacy
FuelWise Studio does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.
